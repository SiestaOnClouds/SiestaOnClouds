## 🚀 Featured Project

### DigiStamp

**DigiStamp** is a multi-tenant digital loyalty platform designed for restaurants, cafés, and other customer-facing businesses.

It replaces traditional paper stamp cards with a digital system combining:

- 📱 QR-based loyalty interactions
- 💳 Apple & Google Wallet integration
- 📡 RFID / NFC terminals
- 📊 Customer segmentation and analytics
- 🎯 Campaign and reward management
- 🔐 Time-limited and single-use authentication
- 💰 Subscription and licensing infrastructure
- 🏢 Multi-tenant SaaS architecture

The project combines **web software and physical hardware**, including ESP32-based terminal development.

**Stack:** `Java` · `Spring Boot` · `SQL` · `REST APIs` · `ESP32` · `Embedded Software`
